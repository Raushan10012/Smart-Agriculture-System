Host Crop: Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum)
Diagnosed Disease: Early Blight
Diagnostic Confidence: 96.4%
Causal Pathogen Alternaria solani (Fungal)
Severity Rating Moderate (Level 3/5)
Visual Symptoms & Manifestation:

Target-like dark brown concentric rings surrounded by yellow chlorotic halos on older leaves.

🌱 Organic & Biological Treatment:

Spray bio-fungicide Bacillus subtilis or cold-pressed neem oil (5 ml/L). Prune lower leaves touching soil.

🧪 Targeted Chemical Intervention:

Apply Chlorothalonil 75 WP (2g/L) or Azoxystrobin 23 SC (1 ml/L). Repeat after 10-12 days if rainfall occurs.

🛡️ Preventive Protocol for Next Season:

Implement 3-year crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops. Use drip irrigation instead of overhead sprinklers.
